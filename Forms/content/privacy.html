Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

Karamah Collective ry (FO number 3649490-3) is a Finnish non-profit association supporting Muslims in Finland through practical guidance and compassionate connections. This policy explains how personal data is processed through Karamah Collective Forms.

2. Information We Collect

Each form collects only the answers, files, and optional contact details its creator requests. If a form requires sign-in, Firebase Authentication verifies your identity; the form tells you when your email is stored with a response.

3. Legal Basis

We process submitted data to provide the activity, registration, service, or response you requested, on the basis of consent, legitimate interests, or another lawful basis stated by the form organiser.

4. How We Use Information

Responses are used only for the stated purpose of the form and by authorised Karamah Collective administrators managing it. We never sell or rent personal information for marketing.

5. Storage and Security

Responses are sent over HTTPS and stored in access-controlled services. Uploaded files and responses are not publicly downloadable. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

6. Retention

We keep information only as long as necessary for the form’s purpose, legal obligations, and essential records. The organiser may set a different retention period where this is communicated in the form.

7. Your Rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or withdrawal of consent. Contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Public and Link-only Forms

Public forms appear on the Forms home page. Link-only forms are not listed, but anybody who receives the link can access them; please do not forward an invitation link without the organiser’s permission.
