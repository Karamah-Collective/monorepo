Sponsored Pin Options

Reference — Regular Pin
This is what all non-sponsored pins look like. Sponsored options must stand out from this.
Regular Pin
White border, subtle drop shadow. The baseline.
Basic Tier
Subtle differentiation — enough to notice, not enough to dominate the map.
B1 — Gold Border (current)
Gold replaces white border. Subtle but doesn't stand out enough.
B2 — Thick Gold Border
Thicker (3.5px) + lighter gold. More visible than B1.
B3 — White Border + Gold Ring
Keeps familiar white border. Gold ring OUTSIDE — visible contrast layer.
B4 — Double Border (white + gold)
White inner border, gold outline. Clean two-tone ring effect.
B5 — Star Badge
Normal pin + small gold star badge on corner. Unambiguous "sponsored" signal.
B6 — Larger Pin
42px instead of 38px. Bigger = more prominent, same white border.
B7 — Gold Shadow
White border, but shadow is warm gold instead of grey. Subtle warm glow.
B8 — Bright Gold Border
3px border in bright gold (#f0c040). Maximum border visibility.
Featured Tier
Clearly stands out — static glow/ring effects, no animation.
F1 — Gold Border + Glow (current)
Gold border, static gold box-shadow ring + spread glow.
F2 — White Border + Gold Glow
Keeps the familiar white border. Gold ring + glow outside. Best of both worlds.
F3 — Double Ring + Wide Glow
White inner border, gold outline, plus wide soft glow. Very premium.
F4 — Larger + Light Gold Glow
44px pin, light gold border, gold ring glow. Size + glow = maximum visual weight.
F5 — Bright Gold + Triple Glow
Bright gold border (#f0c040), 3-layer glow (ring + mid + wide). Eye-catching.
F6 — White Border + Warm Halo
No hard ring — just a wide warm golden halo. Soft and elegant.
F7 — Gradient Gold Border
Gold gradient border (bright to dark gold). Shimmer effect + subtle glow.
F8 — Gold Ring + Star Badge
Gold outer ring + glow, plus a star badge on top-right corner.
F9 — Concentric Rings
Gold border, white gap, gold outer ring, soft glow. Bullseye effect.
Spotlight Tier
Maximum attention — animated effects. Should be the most eye-catching pin on the map.
S1 — Pulsing Glow Ring (current)
Gold border, box-shadow ring pulses in/out. Current implementation.
S2 — White Border + Pulsing Gold
Keeps white border for familiarity. Pulsing gold ring + glow outside.
S3 — Radiating Ripples
Puck-shaped rings radiate outward from the pin. Strong "broadcasting" feel.
S4 — Breathing Scale + Glow
Pin gently grows/shrinks (1.0→1.12) with gold glow. Alive, not aggressive.
S5 — Larger + Bright Gold Pulse
44px pin, bright gold (#f0c040) border, 3-layer pulsing glow. Maximum presence.
S6 — Double Ring + Pulse
White inner border, gold outline, pulsing wide glow. Premium layered look.
S7 — Gradient Border + Star + Pulse
Gradient gold border, pulsing glow, star badge. The kitchen sink option.
S8 — Warm Pulsing Halo
White border, wide warm golden halo that breathes. Elegant, not aggressive.
S9 — Concentric Rings + Pulse
Bright gold border, white gap, gold ring — all pulsing. Bullseye broadcast.
S10 — Shadow-Only Pulse
White border stays constant. Only the golden shadow pulses. Clean and modern.
