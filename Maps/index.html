            Privacy Policy

            Last updated: August 2026

            1. Who We Are

            Manarah is a service by Karamah Collective, a community organisation supporting Finnish Muslims through practical guidance and compassionate connections. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use this app.

            2. Information We Collect

            We collect information only when you voluntarily submit it, sign in, or choose to save/sync items:

            Suggest a Place / Suggest Edit forms:

            • Place name, type, address, Google Maps link, and tags
            • Additional notes you choose to include

            Contact form:

            • Your name and email address (required)
            • Phone number (optional)
            • Your message

            Signing in, ratings, reviews & saved places:

            • Writing a review, or syncing your favourites, saved pins, visited places, and home location across devices, requires signing in through Firebase Authentication with Google, Microsoft, Facebook, or email/password
            • For email/password accounts, Firebase handles sign-up, sign-in, password resets, and email verification. We never see or store your password
            • Your rating (1 to 5), optional review text, and any review photos you choose to upload
            • Saved place IDs, saved pin coordinates/names, visited-place IDs, and home location coordinates/name that you choose to sync
            • A one-way cryptographic hash of your verified email address — never the email address itself — used to link your reviews and synced saved data to your account and to prevent duplicate or abusive submissions

            Place suggestions are anonymous. Reviews and their uploaded photos are shown publicly without displaying your email address or any other sign-in detail.

            3. What We Don't Do

            • No cookies or advertising pixels
            • No behavioural tracking, ad targeting, or user profiling
            • No live location tracking or current-position storage — your current position is used only in your browser for the map and is never sent to our servers. If you set a home location or save a custom pin, that chosen location is stored locally while signed out and synced to your account while signed in
            • We use Cloudflare's approximate IP-based country/city signal only to show the correct regional context, such as a better first map view. It is not stored as your home location.
            • We will never sell personal information or share it for advertising
            • Contact details (name, email, phone, and message) are used solely to respond to your enquiry. They are sent by email through Brevo, so email delivery and SMTP processing are handled by Brevo rather than by us
            • We do not store your email address for reviews or account sync — only a one-way hash, used solely to link them to your account. No plaintext email data is retained from any new review.

            4. Cloudflare Web Analytics

            We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visitors and understand general usage patterns. It is privacy-first by design:

            • No cookies are set
            • No personal data is collected or stored
            • No cross-site tracking or fingerprinting
            • Data is processed by Cloudflare, who already handles all traffic to this site

            Cloudflare's privacy practices are described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

            5. Google reCAPTCHA

            We use Google reCAPTCHA v3 to protect forms from automated abuse. This service may collect hardware and software information. It is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

            6. Third-Party Services

            Manarah relies on the following external services. Each handles its own data independently:

            • Digitransit (HSL & Waltti) — Our primary routing and geocoding API. The HSL endpoint, provided by Helsinki Regional Transport Authority (HSL), covers the Greater Helsinki area. The Waltti endpoint covers the Turku / Föli region. Both are used to calculate public transport routes and to search for addresses in Finland. Governed by the Digitransit API terms.
            • Transitous — Open-source community transit routing, used as a fallback when Digitransit is unavailable. Data sourced from GTFS feeds under open licences.
            • OSRM (OpenStreetMap Routing Machine) — Fallback routing service for walking, cycling, and driving, used when Digitransit is unavailable. Powered by OpenStreetMap data under the ODbL licence.
            • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — Address search and reverse geocoding fallback. Data © OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL.
            • OpenFreeMap — Provides the map tiles and fonts powering the base map. Built on OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data.
            • Esri / ArcGIS — Satellite imagery layer, provided by Esri. Subject to Esri's terms of use.
            • AlAdhan API — Prayer time calculations based on your device's location (using the Muslim World League method). No personal data is stored by this service.
            • MapLibre GL JS — Open-source map rendering library used to display the map.
            • Firebase Authentication — Handles signing in with Google, Microsoft, Facebook, or email/password. We never see or store your password; Firebase issues a verified identity token, which we reduce to a one-way hash before storing synced account data. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and additionally by Microsoft's Privacy Statement when signing in with a Microsoft account and Meta's Privacy Policy when signing in with Facebook.
            • Brevo — Sends Manarah contact form messages to maps@karamahcollective.com by email. Brevo handles the email delivery and SMTP processing for those messages under Brevo's own privacy policy. Contact form messages from new submissions are not stored in our D1 database.

            7. Data Storage & Security

            Place suggestions, reviews, events, wishes, and account-synced saved data are transmitted over HTTPS and stored in a private Cloudflare D1 database accessible only to authorised Karamah Collective team members. Review photos are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket and served publicly only while their review is published. Contact form messages are transmitted over HTTPS to our Cloudflare Function and sent to maps@karamahcollective.com through Brevo; Brevo, not Manarah, handles the email delivery and SMTP processing for those messages. New contact messages are not stored in D1. Contact details are retained only as long as necessary to address your enquiry. Signing in is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google, Microsoft, Facebook, or email/password) — we never receive or store your password. Reviews and synced favourites, saved pins, visited places, and home location are linked to your account using only a one-way hash of your verified email address, retained to enforce review integrity (for example, one review per place per account) and provide account sync — your actual email address is never stored alongside this data. We implement appropriate measures to safeguard data against unauthorised access or loss.

            8. Your Rights (GDPR)

            Under EU data protection law, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any data associated with your submissions. For place suggestions (which are anonymous), please include details that help us identify the relevant entry. For contact form emails, include the email address you used and the approximate submission date. For reviews, review photos, and account-synced saved data, we can locate your data using the email address associated with your account.

            Contact: maps@karamahcollective.com

            9. Children's Privacy

            This service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16.

            10. Featured Places

            Some places in the app are marked as Featured. These are sponsored placements — businesses that support Manarah in exchange for increased visibility. Sponsored places are shown in the app as "Featured" for full transparency.

            What this means in practice:

            • Featured places appear in a highlighted carousel and receive a slight boost in list and search relevance
            • All listings — featured or not — are community-sourced and subject to the same review process
            • Being featured has no effect on a place's halal verification status or tags
            • We do not sell, share, or monetise any user data — featuring is the only form of promotion on this platform
            • We do not guarantee the accuracy of any listing, including featured ones
            • Featured places are clearly labelled in-app so users are always aware

            11. Changes to This Policy

            We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.

            12. Contact

            Questions about this policy? Reach us at maps@karamahcollective.com
