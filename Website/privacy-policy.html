Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

Karamah Collective is a community organisation supporting Muslims in Finland through practical guidance and compassionate connections. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our website at www.karamahcollective.com ("Site"). Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we are responsible for processing your personal data in a transparent and lawful manner.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our contact form:

  • Full name — to address you personally
  • Email address — to respond to your enquiry
  • Phone number (optional) — if you prefer a callback
  • Topic & message — the content of your request
  • Updates preference — whether you actively choose to receive occasional community updates

We do not deploy cookies, analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or any form of behavioural tracking on the Site.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (EU/GDPR)

When you provide personal information through the contact form, the legal basis for processing is one or more of the following:

  • Consent — You explicitly opt in to receiving occasional community updates from us.
  • Legitimate interests — Processing data is necessary for responding to your enquiry, providing services you request, and improving our communications, provided that your rights and freedoms are not overridden.

If you have consented to receive updates, you may withdraw that consent at any time by using the unsubscribe option included in our update emails or by contacting us directly.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information exclusively to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or request
  • Send occasional program, event, volunteer, and community news only if you opt in
  • Improve our services based on the types of requests received
  • Communicate with you for administrative and support purposes connected to your request

"Keep me updated" means occasional Karamah Collective news, events, programs, volunteer opportunities, and community announcements. We do not use this consent to send third-party advertising or unrelated promotional emails.

We will never sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Sharing Personal Information with Partners

We may share personal data with trusted partners and service providers only as necessary to support the services of Karamah Collective (e.g., email delivery platforms, mailing services). We use Brevo to deliver contact-form notification emails and may use it to send opt-in update emails. Brevo's privacy policy is available at brevo.com/legal/privacypolicy/. These partners are contractually limited to use your personal data only within the scope of Karamah Collective's activities and are bound to protect your information.

We will not share your personal information for external marketing or unrelated advertising without your explicit consent.

6. Data Storage & Security

Form submissions are securely transmitted over HTTPS and delivered to authorised team members by email through Brevo. If you actively opt in to updates, we also store only your name, email address, phone number, reCAPTCHA score, and submission date in a private Google Sheet used as our updates opt-in register. We use Google reCAPTCHA v3 solely to protect the form from automated abuse — this service is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

We implement appropriate organisational and technical measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

7. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only in our email systems for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and preserve necessary records. If you opt in to updates, the limited opt-in register is retained until you unsubscribe, withdraw consent, or request deletion. You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at contact@karamahcollective.com.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including GDPR for individuals in the EU/EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent to processing where consent was the lawful basis
  • Object to certain processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@karamahcollective.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect, use, or maintain personal data of anyone under 16. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 16 without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that data.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When updates are made, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
