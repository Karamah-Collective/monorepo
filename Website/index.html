About

Who we are · Why we exist · How we work

Karamah Collective supports Muslims in Finland through clear information, practical guidance, and compassionate connections. We focus on removing barriers to key life events and community needs, while cultivating spaces that honour, faith, and dignity.

Divine Guidance

Ensuring all activities align with Islamic principles.

Sustainable Growth

Building systems that create lasting positive impact.

Eternal Perspective

Focusing on long-term spiritual and community benefits.

Providing Muslims in Finland with the knowledge, support, and tools to be informed, empowered, and connected in matters of faith and society.

We bridge the knowledge gap in the Muslim community with tangible solutions and outreach, while staying true to our Islamic values.

Focus areas

FLAGSHIP PROGRAM

Janazah Initiative

Vision · Mission · Approach

Vision

Establish comprehensive and accessible Islamic end-of-life services across Finland.

Mission

Support bereaved families, develop facilities, secure funding, and grow participation in Islamic burial services.

Approach

Community research, knowledge development, resource creation, and infrastructure support.

Muslim population in Finland is estimated at 120,000+. Only 22,260 were registered as Muslims (2022). Many are therefore not registered as Muslims, which can affect how authorities process end-of-life burial arrangements.

Why this work matters
  • Lack of centralised information
  • Limited infrastructure (washing spaces, equipment, access)
  • Economic burden: Death is an expensive affair in Finland
Success metrics
  • Families assisted & quality of service
  • Community engagement & resource accessibility
  • Infrastructure development progress
Short-term goals
  • Distribute comprehensive information packets
  • Create educational material about Islamic burial practices
  • Establish initial community partnerships
Long-term goals
  • Secure sustainable funding streams
  • Develop dedicated Islamic burial infrastructure
  • Build relationships with relevant authorities
What we aim to provide
In development
  • Immediate next-steps checklist and coordination guidance
  • Orientation to local processes in Finland (where to start, who to contact)
  • Community connections for practical help and volunteering
  • Advocacy and infrastructure work for long-term improvement

We offer community information and coordination support. We do not provide legal services or official religious rulings. For medical emergencies, contact emergency services.

COMMUNITY TOOL

Halal Finder

Discover · Navigate · Connect

Discover

Find mosques, halal restaurants, grocery stores, and community services across Finland on an interactive map.

Navigate

Get directions via public transit, walking, or cycling with real-time schedules and prayer time integration.

Connect

Community-verified listings you can search, filter, and suggest edits to — built by and for the community.

Halal Finder is our open, community-powered map covering the Helsinki metropolitan area and expanding across Finland. It helps Muslims and newcomers locate mosques, halal food, and essential services — all verified by the community.

What you can find
  • Mosques & prayer spaces with service details
  • Halal restaurants, cafés, and grocery stores
  • Islamic centres & community organisations
  • Halal-friendly businesses and services
Features
  • Search & filter by category and tags
  • Public transit & walking directions
  • Daily prayer times for your location
  • Suggest edits & add new places
How it works
  • Browse the map or search for a specific place or category
  • Filter by tags like daily prayers, Jummah, sisters section, and more
  • Get directions with transit schedules or walking routes
  • Suggest edits or new places via the built-in form

Halal Finder relies on community contributions. Listings are informational and may not always be up to date. Always verify details directly with the business.
