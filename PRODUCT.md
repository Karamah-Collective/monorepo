# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Muslims and community members in Finland use Manarah on phones and
desktop browsers while looking for halal food, prayer spaces, services,
events, transit directions, and trustworthy community knowledge. Contributors
also add places, corrections, ratings, reviews, and photos in short focused
workflows.

## Product Purpose

Manarah is a spatial community utility that makes Muslim-friendly places
and services easy to discover, evaluate, reach, and improve collaboratively.
Success means the map stays primary, information is quickly scannable, and a
visitor can complete the next task without navigating ornamental interface.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and community-minded. The experience should feel warm,
restrained, structured, tactile, and quietly premium rather than corporate or
playful.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS styling, excessive empty space, scattered alignment,
decorative cards, glassmorphism, HUD-like controls, heavy shadows, neon color,
oversized typography, unmodified framework components, and UI whose visual
weight competes with the map or the user's task.

## Design Principles

1. Make the current task obvious and compact.
2. Build hierarchy through exact alignment, spacing, typography, and tone.
3. Preserve map context and use familiar product affordances.
4. Keep one primary action per surface and make secondary actions quiet.
5. Treat community-authored information with clarity, safety, and respect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, reduced motion,
dark mode, color-independent status communication, and practical touch targets.
Responsive layouts must remain usable without hover or horizontal overflow at
360 px, while following the app's documented compact phone typography.
